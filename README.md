Bring the Omegaverse to the medieval world of Crusader Kings III!
This mod adds an Omegaverse-inspired secondary gender system to CK3, featuring genetic inheritance, male pregnancy, custom pregnancy mechanics, and heats and ruts.

---

## Features
### Secondary Genders
Characters can be **Alpha**, **Beta**, or **Omega**, with an additional genetic subtype of **Dominant**, **Standard**, or **Recessive**.
For Betas, anything other than Standard presents as **Unstable Beta**.
All characters are assigned their secondary gender and genetic subtype when the game is initialized in the lobby.
Children inherit their genetics from their biological parents, with probabilities determined by the combination of parental traits. When parental genetics aren't available, fallbacks are used.

### Hidden Genetics
A child's secondary gender remains hidden until adolescence.
At **age 10**, children begin receiving events and trait description updates that hint at their genetics.
At **age 16**, their secondary gender is revealed according to their inherited genetics.

### Pregnancy & Birth
Male pregnancy comes to medieval CK3! Custom pregnancy and birth mechanics allow combinations that vanilla CK3 doesn't normally support.
Pregnancies can result in normal births, premature endings, illness, and other complications. Children are assigned biological parentage and dynastic relationships accordingly.

### Cycles, Heats & Ruts
Adult characters experience heats or ruts, with their current cycle reflected in their traits and descriptions. These cycles also affect fertility.

## Secondary Gender Genetics (Alpha / Beta / Omega)
| Parent Pair | Alpha | Beta | Omega |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Alpha + Omega | 40% | 20% | 40% |
| Alpha + Beta | 55% | 35% | 10% |
| Beta + Omega | 10% | 35% | 55% |
